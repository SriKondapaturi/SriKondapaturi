## About Me

I'm an **ASIC Physical Design Engineer** with hands-on RTL-to-GDSII experience focused on floorplanning, partitioning, power and clock distribution, and timing closure across multi-clock-domain SoCs and multi-chiplet architectures. I'm skilled in **Cadence Innovus, Synopsys PrimeTime, SpyGlass CDC, JasperGold, and Calibre**, with strong **Perl, TCL, Python, and C++** automation for PD flow development and ECO closure. My work is complemented by **SystemVerilog/UVM verification** background and graduate coursework in VLSI and computer architecture. Currently pursuing my **M.S. in ECE at California State University, Fresno** — Cadence RTL-to-GDSII and Synopsys PrimeTime certified.

- 🔧 Currently exploring **advanced-node Physical Design** and **multi-chiplet SoC architectures**
- 🚀 Open to **full-time ASIC Physical Design / DFT Engineer** roles
- ⚡ Passionate about **timing closure, low-power design, and clock domain crossings**
- 💡 Ask me about **Innovus, PrimeTime, SpyGlass CDC, UVM verification, or RTL-to-GDSII flow**
- 🛠️ Love turning **RTL into manufacturable silicon**
- 📍 Based in **California, USA**
- 📫 Reach me at **srigovardhan96@gmail.com**

## Featured Projects

### 1. Multi-Chiplet SoC with DVFS, CDC, and Hierarchical Power Management (RTL-to-GDSII)
> Heterogeneous chiplet SoC: Application Processor + NPU + shared L2 over NoC with 12+ async clock domains.
- 🧠 **100% structural CDC and RDC closure** using SpyGlass CDC and JasperGold
- ⚙️ Full **RTL-to-GDSII in Cadence Innovus** + signoff STA in PrimeTime across MCMM corners
- 🔋 Per-chiplet **DVFS** with clock gating, multi-Vt cells, and voltage scaling

### 2. UVM-Based Verification of Hack 16-bit CPU and ALU (Cadence Xcelium)
> Complete UVM environment with reusable, factory-driven components and golden-reference checking.
- 🧱 Built **driver, monitor, scoreboard, sequencer, agent**, and factory-based tests
- 📊 Achieved **functional + code + toggle** coverage closure
- 🐛 **SVA-driven** bug triage and golden-reference scoreboarding

### 3. High-Speed Serial Link Encoder with 8b/10b Encoding and Scrambler (SystemVerilog)
> Industry-standard line coding and scrambling for high-speed serial links.
- 🔁 **8b/10b encoder/decoder** with running disparity tracking and K-character handling
- 🔀 Self-synchronizing scrambler (**x^58 + x^39 + 1**, IEEE 802.3 64b/66b)
- ✅ Verified across **10K+ randomized vectors**; synthesized in Design Compiler, STA in PrimeTime

### 4. DFT-Enabled FSM Traffic Light Controller with Scan, BIST, and ATPG (Synopsys Flow)
> End-to-end DFT methodology demonstrated on an FSM-based controller.
- 🎯 Achieved **90.93% stuck-at fault coverage** using Synopsys TetraMAX
- 🧬 Integrated **scan chain + BIST (LFSR/MISR)** architecture
- ⏱️ **SDF-annotated** gate-level simulation across shift / capture / mission modes
