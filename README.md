## About Me

I'm a recent **M.S. graduate in Electrical and Computer Engineering** from **California State University, Fresno**, passionate about **VLSI backend design** — from **Physical Design** (P&R, STA, timing closure, low-power design) to **Design-for-Test (DFT)** methodologies (scan insertion, ATPG, MBIST, JTAG).

I love the journey of transforming an RTL description into a manufacturable, timing-clean piece of silicon — every nanosecond of slack and every micron of routing matters.

- 🔬 Currently exploring **advanced node Physical Design** and **DFT methodologies**
- 💼 Open to **full-time roles** in **VLSI Physical Design / DFT**
- ⚙️ Love turning **RTL into manufacturable silicon**
- 💡 Ask me about **timing closure**, **congestion**, or **scan compression**
